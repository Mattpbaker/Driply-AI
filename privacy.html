Privacy Policy

Effective June 20, 2026

Local-first by default. Mog AI keeps your ratings and photos on your device, with a copy in your private iCloud. Your data is for your eyes only — it is never made public or shared with other users.

What We Collect

When you use Mog AI, we collect the following data to operate the app:

  • Name — your display name, collected during onboarding and stored on your device and in iCloud to identify your profile within the app.
  • Photos — selfies you submit for AI face rating, stored on your device and optionally in iCloud as part of your rating history. Profile pictures are also stored in iCloud.
  • Social handles — your Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter/X handles, if you choose to add them to your own profile. Stored only in your private iCloud and shown to you on your personal profile within the app; they are not published or shared with anyone else.
  • Profile bio — optional bio text you add to your own profile. Stored only in your private iCloud and shown to you on your personal profile within the app; it is not published or shared with anyone else.
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated for your account, used to link your ratings and profile together across the app and iCloud.
  • Device ID — a device-level identifier collected by RevenueCat (our subscription provider) to manage your subscription and restore purchases across devices.
  • Purchase history — your subscription status and in-app purchase records, managed by RevenueCat to determine your Pro entitlement and consumable balances.
  • Product interaction — how you interact with subscription offers and paywalls, tracked by RevenueCat to operate subscription features and measure offer performance.

How Your Photos Are Processed

Before your first photo is sent for analysis, we ask for your explicit consent. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings → Privacy & Data, after which no further photos are sent.

When you submit a selfie for an AI face rating, your photo is sent over an encrypted connection to xAI (the company behind Grok) for real-time analysis. We have configured this integration with storage disabled — xAI processes your photo in memory and discards it the moment the rating response is returned. xAI does not retain your photo, does not store it on their servers, and does not use it to train their models.

Your photo is then stored on your device as part of your rating history, and a copy may be stored only in your private iCloud (CloudKit) database. Your ratings and photos are never made public or shared with other users.

Third-Party Services

Mog AI uses the following third-party services. Each receives only the data necessary to perform their function:

  • xAI / Grok (api.x.ai) — processes face photos for AI rating. Real-time only; no data is stored or used for training. xAI Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions, in-app purchases, and consumables. Collects device ID, user ID, purchase history, and paywall interaction data. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iCloud / CloudKit — stores your profile, rating history, and photos in your private iCloud database. Governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or data brokers. None of your data is sold or shared for advertising purposes.

How Data Is Used

  • Deliver AI face ratings and appearance coaching features.
  • Display your profile and ratings to you within the app.
  • Provide your personal analysis, coaching, and progress tracking.
  • Manage your Pro subscription, consumable ratings, and streak revival purchases.

Your data is never used for advertising, never sold to third parties, and never linked with data from other apps or services for tracking purposes.

Data Linked to You

The following data types are linked to your identity within Mog AI:

  • Name
  • Photos and profile picture
  • Social handles and profile bio (stored only in your private profile)
  • User ID
  • Device ID (via RevenueCat)
  • Purchase history and subscription status
  • Paywall and offer interaction (via RevenueCat)

Your Controls

  • Delete your rating history and photos in-app.
  • Delete your ratings and profile data at any time.
  • Withdraw your AI photo consent anytime in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Manage your subscription and restore purchases through your Apple ID settings or in-app.

To request deletion of all data associated with your account, email us at contact@mogai.app.

Retention

  • Photos and ratings stored on your device are retained until you delete them.
  • Photos and ratings stored in iCloud are retained until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Photos sent to xAI for analysis are discarded immediately after processing — zero retention.
  • Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat in accordance with their privacy policy.

Children and Age

Mog AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Age eligibility is enforced through App Store age ratings and device parental controls.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
