Privacy Policy

Effective February 28, 2026

Local-first by default. Mog AI keeps your ratings and photos on your device unless you explicitly choose to share them. You are always in control of what goes public.

What We Collect

  • Account/profile basics used by app features (display name, profile metadata).
  • Private ratings/photos stored locally unless you choose to share.
  • Shared/public content you explicitly publish (ratings, interactions).
  • Operational events for safety and reliability (for example moderation actions, export/delete failures, migration status).

How Data Is Used

  • Provide AI appearance-feedback features and app functionality.
  • Operate public features when you share content.
  • Enforce trust and safety policies (reporting, blocking, abuse controls).
  • Maintain service reliability and investigate abuse/security issues.

Your Controls

  • Export your data in-app.
  • Delete local on-device data in-app.
  • Delete shared cloud/public footprint in-app.
  • Unshare/delete shared content for immediate remote removal flow.

Retention

  • Private local data is retained on your device until you delete it.
  • Shared/public content remains visible until deleted or unshared.
  • Legacy private cloud copies are purged by migration where applicable.

Children and Age

Platform-level age controls are handled through app-store systems and device settings.

Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact support through official Mog AI support channels.
